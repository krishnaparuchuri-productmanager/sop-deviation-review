SOP-004: Raw Material Testing
Version: 2.7 | Effective Date: 2025-02-10 | Review Date: 2027-02-10
Department: Quality Control | Author: QC Manager

================================================================================
SECTION 1: PURPOSE
================================================================================
This SOP defines the procedures for receipt, sampling, testing, quarantine,
and release or rejection of raw materials (active pharmaceutical ingredients,
excipients, packaging components, and processing aids). No raw material shall
be used in manufacturing unless it has been tested and formally released by
Quality Control. Use of untested, quarantined, or rejected materials in
manufacturing constitutes a critical deviation with immediate patient safety
implications.

================================================================================
SECTION 2: SCOPE
================================================================================
This SOP applies to all raw materials, starting materials, excipients, reagents
used in product manufacture, primary and secondary packaging components, and
processing aids that come into direct or indirect contact with the product.
It covers all stages from goods receipt to formal release or rejection.

================================================================================
SECTION 3: RESPONSIBILITIES
================================================================================
3.1 Warehouse / Goods Receipt Team
    - Assign a unique Goods Receipt (GR) number to every incoming material.
    - Apply a QUARANTINE label to all incoming materials immediately upon receipt.
    - Do not accept materials whose containers are damaged, unsealed, or show
      signs of contamination. Notify QC and QA immediately in such cases.
    - Store materials under quarantine in the designated quarantine area.

3.2 QC Sampling Analyst
    - Sample materials per the approved sampling plan within 2 business days
      of goods receipt.
    - Maintain sample chain of custody per SOP-001 (Sample Management).

3.3 QC Testing Analyst
    - Perform all tests specified in the approved material specification.
    - Record all results in the raw data notebook and LIMS on the day of testing.
    - Report any out-of-specification (OOS) result to QC Supervisor immediately.

3.4 QC Supervisor
    - Review and approve test results in LIMS before label change is authorized.
    - Initiate OOS investigation per SOP-007 for any failing result.

3.5 Quality Assurance (QA)
    - Provide final release or rejection authorization for all raw materials.
    - Approve use of any material under a conditional or temporary waiver.
    - Investigate use of untested or rejected material in manufacturing.

================================================================================
SECTION 4: PROCEDURE
================================================================================
4.1 Goods Receipt and Quarantine
    4.1.1 Upon delivery, verify the supplier's certificate of analysis (CoA)
          against the purchase order for: material name, grade, specification
          version, lot number, and quantity.
    4.1.2 Inspect containers for: physical damage, seal integrity, labeling
          completeness, and evidence of pest or moisture ingress.
    4.1.3 Reject the delivery without sampling if containers show signs of
          tampering, damage, or contamination. Document on the Goods Receipt
          Non-Conformance form and notify QA within 2 hours.
    4.1.4 Apply a QUARANTINE label to all containers that pass the visual
          inspection. Move to the designated quarantine zone.
    4.1.5 Enter goods receipt details into the inventory system within 4 hours
          of receipt. Materials must not leave quarantine without QC authorization.

4.2 Sampling
    4.2.1 Sample according to the approved sampling plan. The plan specifies:
          number of containers to sample, sample size, sampling tool, and
          required personal protective equipment.
    4.2.2 For APIs and sterile excipients, sampling must be performed in a
          controlled environment (Grade C minimum) using aseptic technique.
    4.2.3 After sampling, re-seal all sampled containers with a tamper-evident
          closure and re-label with the date sampled and sampler ID.
    4.2.4 Submit samples to QC lab with a Sample Submission Form. The form
          must reference the GR number and approved specification version.

4.3 Testing
    4.3.1 All raw materials must be tested against the full current approved
          specification. Do not test against a superseded specification version.
    4.3.2 Required tests for APIs always include: identification (IR, HPLC,
          or wet chemistry), assay (potency), purity (related substances),
          and residual solvents where applicable.
    4.3.3 Required tests for excipients include at minimum: identification
          and any specification limits for safety-critical parameters.
    4.3.4 Supplier CoA results may support — but never replace — in-house
          identity testing. Identity must always be confirmed in-house.
    4.3.5 Record all raw data (instrument printouts, calculations, observations)
          in the bound raw data notebook or LIMS electronic record on the day
          of testing. Do not transcribe results from scratch paper or memory.

4.4 Release and Rejection
    4.4.1 When all results pass the approved specification, the QC Supervisor
          approves the batch in LIMS and authorizes replacement of the
          QUARANTINE label with an APPROVED label.
    4.4.2 When any result fails the specification, initiate an OOS investigation
          (SOP-007) before making a disposition decision. Do not reject without
          investigation unless the failure is unambiguous and the material is
          clearly non-conforming (e.g., wrong material delivered).
    4.4.3 When a material is rejected, apply a REJECTED label to all containers,
          move to the segregated reject area, and notify QA and Procurement.
    4.4.4 Rejected materials must not re-enter the manufacturing area under
          any circumstances, including for non-GMP or research purposes,
          without written QA authorization.
    4.4.5 Materials may not be used in manufacturing while under QUARANTINE
          status or under OOS investigation. Any exception requires a
          written waiver approved by QA and the Qualified Person (QP).

4.5 Conditional Release (Waiver)
    4.5.1 In exceptional circumstances, a material may be conditionally released
          under a documented waiver if: testing is in progress, identity has been
          confirmed, and a risk assessment supports limited use.
    4.5.2 Any batch manufactured using a conditionally released material is placed
          on hold pending final material test results.
    4.5.3 If the material subsequently fails testing, the manufactured batch must
          be quarantined and QA must perform a full impact assessment.

SECTION 5: DEVIATION CRITERIA AND ESCALATION TRIGGERS
================================================================================
5.1 The following events constitute a deviation and must be documented within
    24 hours of discovery:

    (a) Raw material used in manufacturing while under QUARANTINE status.
    (b) Raw material used in manufacturing before identity testing was completed.
    (c) Raw material used in manufacturing after it was formally REJECTED.
    (d) Sampling not completed within 2 business days of goods receipt.
    (e) Testing performed against a superseded or unapproved specification.
    (f) Supplier CoA accepted as sole evidence of identity without in-house test.
    (g) Damaged or unsealed containers accepted and sampled without QA notification.
    (h) APPROVED label applied without QC Supervisor sign-off in LIMS.
    (i) Rejected material found in the manufacturing area or in-process.

5.2 Immediate Escalation to QA is required for:

    (a) Any raw material used in manufacturing that was subsequently found
        to have failed identity, assay, or purity testing.
    (b) Use of a rejected or quarantined API in any manufacturing operation.
    (c) Discovery of rejected material mixed with approved material in storage.
    (d) Any batch manufactured using a material that was not tested in-house
        for identity.
    (e) Out-of-specification potency result on an API already incorporated
        into an in-process or finished batch.
    (f) Suspected adulteration or counterfeiting of any incoming material.
    (g) Use of an expired raw material (past retest date) in manufacturing.

5.3 Risk Classification Guide:
    HIGH:   Any material used in manufacturing that failed or was not tested
            for identity or potency; rejected material found in manufacturing;
            suspected counterfeit or adulterated material; expired API used.
    MEDIUM: Quarantine material used under an undocumented waiver; sampling
            delay beyond 2 days with material still in quarantine; minor
            documentation error on CoA verification with all tests passed.
    LOW:    Administrative labeling error corrected before sampling; late
            goods receipt entry with no impact on testing timeline.

================================================================================
SECTION 6: DOCUMENTATION REQUIREMENTS
================================================================================
6.1 Goods Receipt Record — completed within 4 hours of delivery.
6.2 Goods Receipt Non-Conformance Form — for any rejected delivery.
6.3 Sample Submission Form — accompanies every sample to the QC lab.
6.4 Raw Data Notebook / LIMS Record — completed on day of testing.
6.5 Certificate of Analysis (CoA) — filed with the batch record for each lot.
6.6 Material Release / Rejection Record — QC Supervisor sign-off in LIMS.
6.7 OOS Investigation Report — initiated within 2 hours of any failing result.
6.8 Conditional Release Waiver — signed by QA and QP before material use.
