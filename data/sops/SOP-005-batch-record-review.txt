SOP-005: Batch Record Review
Version: 3.0 | Effective Date: 2025-04-01 | Review Date: 2027-04-01
Department: Quality Assurance | Author: QA Director

================================================================================
SECTION 1: PURPOSE
================================================================================
This SOP defines the requirements for the review, verification, and approval of
batch manufacturing records (BMRs) and batch packaging records (BPRs) prior to
batch disposition. A thorough batch record review ensures that each batch was
manufactured and tested in compliance with the approved process, specifications,
and regulatory requirements, and that all deviations have been identified,
documented, investigated, and appropriately resolved before a release decision
is made. No batch shall be released unless the batch record review is complete
and the Qualified Person (QP) has provided written authorization.

================================================================================
SECTION 2: SCOPE
================================================================================
This SOP applies to all batch manufacturing records and batch packaging records
for finished pharmaceutical products manufactured at this facility, including
products for clinical trials, commercial release, and stability studies. It
covers paper-based records, electronic records, and hybrid systems.

================================================================================
SECTION 3: RESPONSIBILITIES
================================================================================
3.1 Production / Manufacturing Operator
    - Complete all entries in the BMR at the time of each operation.
    - Correct errors using the approved method: single strikethrough, date,
      initials, reason. Never use correction fluid or overwrite entries.
    - Obtain a second-person verification signature for all critical steps
      as designated in the BMR.

3.2 Production Supervisor
    - Review the completed BMR for completeness before submitting to QA.
    - Ensure all in-process deviations are documented and a deviation report
      number is referenced in the BMR before submission.

3.3 QC Analyst / QC Manager
    - Complete all analytical test results in LIMS before batch record review.
    - Ensure the Certificate of Analysis (CoA) is attached to the batch record.
    - Flag any OOS result to QA before the batch record is submitted for review.

3.4 QA Reviewer
    - Perform the formal batch record review per Section 4.
    - Document all findings in the Batch Record Review Checklist.
    - Escalate critical findings to the QA Manager immediately.
    - Never approve a batch record that has unresolved deviations.

3.5 Qualified Person (QP)
    - Provide final written certification of each batch before release.
    - The QP may not certify a batch where the batch record review has not
      been completed and signed off by a QA Reviewer.

================================================================================
SECTION 4: PROCEDURE
================================================================================
4.1 Batch Record Completeness Check
    4.1.1 Verify that all pages of the BMR are present and in the correct order.
          Page numbers and total page count must be confirmed.
    4.1.2 Confirm all required fields are completed. Blank fields are
          not acceptable — fields that do not apply must be marked "N/A"
          with the reviewer's initials.
    4.1.3 Verify that all critical process steps have a second-person
          verification signature where required by the BMR template.
    4.1.4 Confirm all in-process dates and times are logical and consistent
          (e.g., blending end time is after blending start time; no step
          recorded before the preceding step was completed).
    4.1.5 Verify that all in-process test results are within approved limits
          and are signed by the performing analyst and the QC Supervisor.

4.2 Error Correction Review
    4.2.1 Review all corrections made to the BMR. Each correction must follow
          the approved method: single line through the error so original text
          is legible, correction written adjacent, initialed, dated, and
          reason provided.
    4.2.2 Corrections made using correction fluid, overwriting, or erasure
          are not acceptable under any circumstances and require a formal
          deviation report.
    4.2.3 Multiple corrections clustered around a single step may indicate
          a systemic issue. Escalate to QA Manager if more than 3 corrections
          appear for the same process step.

4.3 Deviation and Exception Review
    4.3.1 Confirm that every deviation from the batch record instructions has
          a corresponding deviation report number recorded in the BMR.
    4.3.2 Verify that each referenced deviation report has been: opened,
          investigated, root-cause identified, impact assessed, and closed
          (or approved for batch release with open CAPA) before review.
    4.3.3 A batch with an unresolved deviation of HIGH risk classification
          must not be released until the deviation is closed or the QP
          provides a written justification for release with open CAPA.
    4.3.4 Verify that any deviation occurring during a critical step
          (e.g., sterilization, aseptic filling, terminal sterilization)
          has been reviewed and signed off by QA before the next step
          was permitted to proceed.

4.4 Analytical Results Review
    4.4.1 Confirm that all finished product test results are present in LIMS
          and that a printed CoA is attached to or referenced in the batch record.
    4.4.2 Verify all results are within specification. Any OOS result must be
          accompanied by a closed OOS investigation report before the batch
          can be reviewed for release.
    4.4.3 Confirm that the analytical methods used are current, approved versions.
          Results generated using superseded analytical methods require QA review.
    4.4.4 Verify that all instruments used for release testing were calibrated
          and within their calibration period at the time of testing (cross-
          reference with calibration records per SOP-003).

4.5 Yield Reconciliation
    4.5.1 Calculate theoretical yield, actual yield, and percentage yield.
          Confirm the actual yield is within the approved yield range defined
          in the product's manufacturing process description.
    4.5.2 Investigate yield outside the approved range before batch disposition.
          Unexplained low yield may indicate diversion, process failure, or
          weighing errors. Escalate unexplained low yield to QA immediately.
    4.5.3 Record reconciled quantities for all critical starting materials,
          packaging components, and printed labels. Unaccounted label
          discrepancies must be investigated and resolved before release.

4.6 Batch Record Approval
    4.6.1 Upon completion of the review, the QA Reviewer signs and dates the
          Batch Record Review Checklist, noting any issues found and their
          resolution status.
    4.6.2 If the batch record is approved: forward to the QP for certification.
    4.6.3 If the batch record is rejected: document deficiencies, return to
          Production or QC for correction, and re-review the corrected record.
    4.6.4 Under no circumstances may a QA Reviewer approve a batch record
          that they personally completed entries in during manufacturing.

================================================================================
SECTION 5: DEVIATION CRITERIA AND ESCALATION TRIGGERS
================================================================================
5.1 The following events constitute a deviation and must be documented within
    24 hours of discovery:

    (a) Blank fields in a submitted BMR not marked "N/A."
    (b) Missing second-person verification signature for a critical step.
    (c) Date/time inconsistency in process records.
    (d) Correction using correction fluid, overwriting, or erasure.
    (e) Deviation referenced in BMR without a corresponding open deviation report.
    (f) Batch record approved by the QA Reviewer who also made BMR entries.
    (g) QP batch certification issued before batch record review was completed.
    (h) Analytical result in the batch record not matching the LIMS record.

5.2 Immediate Escalation to QA Manager / QP is required for:

    (a) Evidence of falsification or backdating of any batch record entry.
    (b) Batch released to market with an unresolved HIGH-risk deviation.
    (c) Missing entries for a critical manufacturing step (sterilization,
        aseptic fill, lyophilization) with no corroborating documentation.
    (d) Yield reconciliation showing unaccounted material exceeding 1% of
        batch size for a controlled substance or high-potency API.
    (e) Discovery that a batch was certified by the QP based on an
        incomplete or materially incorrect batch record.
    (f) Discrepancy between the batch record and the corresponding electronic
        manufacturing execution system (MES) data.
    (g) Post-release discovery of a batch record error that may affect
        product safety, efficacy, or regulatory compliance.

5.3 Risk Classification Guide:
    HIGH:   Evidence of falsification or data integrity breach; batch released
            with unresolved critical deviation; missing critical step records;
            post-release discovery of material record error.
    MEDIUM: Unresolved deviation at time of review (batch on hold); multiple
            correction errors in BMR; yield outside range under investigation;
            OOS result with open investigation.
    LOW:    Minor omission (e.g., missing "N/A" in non-critical field) corrected
            during review; single correction error with adequate documentation.

================================================================================
SECTION 6: DOCUMENTATION REQUIREMENTS
================================================================================
6.1 Batch Manufacturing Record (BMR) — completed at time of each operation.
6.2 Batch Record Review Checklist — completed by QA Reviewer for every batch.
6.3 Certificate of Analysis (CoA) — attached or referenced in batch record.
6.4 Deviation Reports — all referenced deviations filed and status confirmed.
6.5 OOS Investigation Reports — closed or in-progress status confirmed.
6.6 Yield Reconciliation Worksheet — attached to BMR before QA review.
6.7 QP Batch Certification Record — final authorization before release.
6.8 Batch Disposition Record — filed after release or rejection decision.
