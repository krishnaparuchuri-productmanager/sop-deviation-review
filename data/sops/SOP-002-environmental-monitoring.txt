SOP-002: Environmental Monitoring
Version: 2.4 | Effective Date: 2025-03-01 | Review Date: 2027-03-01
Department: Quality Control | Author: Microbiology Lead

================================================================================
SECTION 1: PURPOSE
================================================================================
This SOP defines the requirements for environmental monitoring (EM) of controlled
manufacturing and laboratory areas, including cleanrooms, controlled environments,
and associated support spaces. The purpose is to detect microbiological and
particulate contamination trends, verify the effectiveness of cleaning and
disinfection procedures, and protect product quality and patient safety.

================================================================================
SECTION 2: SCOPE
================================================================================
This SOP covers: active air sampling (using volumetric air samplers), passive
air sampling (settle plates), surface contact sampling (contact plates and
swabs), personnel monitoring (glove and gown sampling), and non-viable particle
counting. It applies to all ISO-classified cleanrooms (ISO 5 through ISO 8)
and Grade A–D areas as classified per EU GMP Annex 1.

================================================================================
SECTION 3: RESPONSIBILITIES
================================================================================
3.1 Microbiologist / EM Technician
    - Execute the EM schedule per the approved Environmental Monitoring Plan.
    - Record all results in the Environmental Monitoring Log on the same day.
    - Immediately notify the QC Supervisor if any result exceeds an Alert Limit.
    - Ensure sampling equipment is calibrated before each use.

3.2 QC Supervisor
    - Review EM results within 1 business day of receipt.
    - Initiate trending review when three consecutive samples approach Alert Limits.
    - Escalate Action Limit exceedances to QA within 2 hours of confirmation.

3.3 Production Supervisor
    - Halt production in the affected area upon notification of an Action Limit
      exceedance until QA provides written clearance to resume.

3.4 Quality Assurance (QA)
    - Investigate all Action Limit exceedances and out-of-specification results.
    - Review and approve Corrective Action and Preventive Action (CAPA) plans.
    - Authorize resumption of manufacturing after an area exceedance event.

================================================================================
SECTION 4: PROCEDURE
================================================================================
4.1 Monitoring Schedule
    4.1.1 Monitoring must be performed per the frequency defined in the approved
          Environmental Monitoring Plan (EMP). Do not skip or defer scheduled
          monitoring without documented justification approved by QA.
    4.1.2 Monitoring must be performed during representative manufacturing
          operations (at-rest and in-operation as defined in the EMP).
    4.1.3 If scheduled monitoring is missed, document the reason in the EM Log
          and notify QC Supervisor the same day. QA must approve any deviation
          from the EMP frequency.

4.2 Active Air Sampling
    4.2.1 Use only calibrated RCS or slit-to-agar volumetric samplers.
          Verify calibration status on the equipment tag before use.
    4.2.2 Sample the specified volume of air (typically 1,000 litres for Grade A/B;
          500 litres for Grade C/D) at each designated sample point.
    4.2.3 Record: sample point ID, date, start time, sampler ID, volume sampled,
          and operator ID.
    4.2.4 Incubate plates at 30–35 °C for 48–72 hours, then 20–25 °C for
          48–72 hours (dual-incubation). Do not skip the dual-incubation step.

4.3 Settle Plates (Passive Air Sampling)
    4.3.1 Expose 90 mm settle plates at designated points for the exposure time
          specified in the EMP (typically 4 hours for Grade A/B).
    4.3.2 Do not exceed maximum exposure time — extended exposure invalidates
          the plate. Record actual exposure start and end times.
    4.3.3 If a plate is accidentally disturbed during exposure, discard it,
          document the event, and replace with a fresh plate if time permits.

4.4 Surface and Contact Sampling
    4.4.1 Take contact plate impressions of critical surfaces (filling needles,
          stopper bowls, conveyor belts) after cleaning and disinfection,
          immediately before production commences.
    4.4.2 Take swab samples from irregular surfaces and hard-to-reach areas
          per the EMP sampling map.
    4.4.3 Allow contact plates to neutralize for 15 minutes before incubation
          when disinfectant residue may be present.

4.5 Personnel Monitoring
    4.5.1 Sample gloves and gown surfaces of Grade A/B personnel after each
          production operation and at exit from the cleanroom.
    4.5.2 Positive personnel monitoring results must be investigated. The
          individual must be re-trained before re-entering a Grade A/B area.

4.6 Alert and Action Limits
    4.6.1 Alert Limits (CFU) — Triggers investigation and increased monitoring:
          Grade A: >0 CFU/m³ (air), >0 CFU/plate (settle), >0 CFU/plate (surface)
          Grade B: >10 CFU/m³, >5 CFU/4h plate, >5 CFU/contact plate
          Grade C: >100 CFU/m³, >50 CFU/4h plate, >25 CFU/contact plate
          Grade D: >200 CFU/m³, >100 CFU/4h plate, >50 CFU/contact plate

    4.6.2 Action Limits (CFU) — Triggers immediate CAPA and QA notification:
          Grade A: >1 CFU (any method)
          Grade B: >50 CFU/m³, >25 CFU/4h plate, >25 CFU/contact plate
          Grade C: >200 CFU/m³, >100 CFU/4h plate, >50 CFU/contact plate
          Grade D: >500 CFU/m³, >200 CFU/4h plate, >100 CFU/contact plate

================================================================================
SECTION 5: DEVIATION CRITERIA AND ESCALATION TRIGGERS
================================================================================
5.1 The following events constitute a deviation and must be documented within
    24 hours of discovery:

    (a) Scheduled EM event missed without prior QA approval.
    (b) Sampling equipment used without current calibration verification.
    (c) Incubation conditions (temperature or duration) outside specification.
    (d) Settle plate exposure time exceeded by more than 10 minutes.
    (e) Monitoring performed outside of the area or time window defined in EMP.
    (f) Results not recorded in the EM Log on the day of sampling.

5.2 Immediate Escalation to QA is required for:

    (a) Any result at or above the Action Limit for any area grade.
    (b) Any Grade A or Grade B exceedance, regardless of magnitude.
    (c) Multiple consecutive Alert Limit exceedances (≥3) at the same location.
    (d) Positive result on a personnel gown or glove sample.
    (e) Discovery of a missed monitoring event for a Grade A/B area.
    (f) Any contamination event coinciding with an active aseptic filling campaign.
    (g) Identification of an objectionable organism (e.g., Burkholderia cepacia,
        Staphylococcus aureus, mould) at any grade level.

5.3 Risk Classification Guide:
    HIGH:   Action Limit exceedance; any Grade A/B exceedance; objectionable
            organism identified; event coinciding with active sterile production.
    MEDIUM: Alert Limit exceedance in Grade C/D; missed monitoring for Grade C/D
            area; incubation deviation with documented temperature records.
    LOW:    Minor procedural deviation (e.g., late log entry) with no impact on
            result validity; settle plate disturbed in Grade D area only.

================================================================================
SECTION 6: DOCUMENTATION REQUIREMENTS
================================================================================
6.1 Environmental Monitoring Log — completed same day as sampling.
6.2 Equipment Calibration Check Log — before each sampling session.
6.3 Incubation Log — temperature and duration recorded for every plate.
6.4 Out-of-Specification (OOS) Report — raised within 2 hours of confirmed OOS.
6.5 CAPA Record — initiated within 5 business days of QA escalation.
6.6 Trending Report — monthly review submitted to QA by the 5th of each month.
