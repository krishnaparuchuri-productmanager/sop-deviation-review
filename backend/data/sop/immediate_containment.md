# SOP-IC-002: Immediate Containment — Actions After a Deviation

**Document ID:** SOP-IC-002
**Version:** 1.8
**Effective Date:** 2025-02-15
**Department:** Quality Assurance / Manufacturing
**Review Cycle:** Biennial

---

## 1. Purpose

This procedure defines the immediate actions required upon discovery of a deviation to prevent further non-conformance, protect product integrity, and preserve evidence needed for investigation. Immediate containment must be initiated before, and independently of, the formal deviation investigation.

---

## 2. Scope

Applies to all personnel in manufacturing, quality control, warehousing, and distribution who discover or are notified of a deviation. Containment actions are required for all Critical and Major deviations as defined in SOP-DC-001.

---

## 3. Immediate Containment Principles

Containment is not investigation. The goal at this stage is to **stop the problem from spreading**, not to determine root cause. Personnel must resist the urge to troubleshoot before containment is complete.

Three actions must occur in parallel as quickly as possible:

1. **Stop** — halt the affected operation or process if continuation would worsen the deviation or destroy evidence.
2. **Secure** — quarantine affected materials, products, equipment, and records. Apply physical quarantine labels immediately.
3. **Notify** — alert the supervisor and QA as defined in SOP-DC-001 Section 5.

---

## 4. Containment Actions by Deviation Type

### 4.1 Product or Material Non-Conformance
Apply a QUARANTINE label to all affected containers immediately. Do not move, blend, or process quarantined material without written QA authorization. Identify any downstream batches that may have incorporated the affected material and quarantine those too.

### 4.2 Equipment Failure or Out-of-Calibration Discovery
Remove the equipment from service and affix an OUT OF SERVICE label. Do not attempt to recalibrate or repair before QA is notified. Identify all batches processed or tested using the equipment since its last confirmed good calibration state.

### 4.3 Environmental Exceedance
Notify QA immediately — do not wait for confirmed colony counts. Halt manufacturing in the affected area if the result is at or above the Action Limit. Do not clean or disinfect until QA authorizes it; premature cleaning destroys microbiological evidence.

### 4.4 Documentation or Data Integrity Issue
Secure the original record and do not make further entries or corrections. Make a certified true copy stored separately. Escalate to QA Manager within 1 hour if falsification or data manipulation is suspected.

---

## 5. Quarantine Label Requirements

A physical quarantine label must include: the word QUARANTINE in red text, the date and time applied, the name of the person applying the label, a brief description of the reason, and the eQMS Deviation Report number once assigned.

---

## 6. Evidence Preservation

Do not destroy, discard, clean, or alter anything associated with the deviation until QA provides written authorization. This includes: equipment, materials, environmental monitoring plates, instrument printouts, electronic records, and any physical area involved in the event.

---

## 7. Responsibilities

- **Operator / Analyst:** Initiate Stop and Secure steps immediately upon discovery. Notify supervisor within 15 minutes.
- **Supervisor:** Confirm containment is in place. Notify QA. Assign a containment owner.
- **QA:** Verify adequacy of containment before authorizing resumption of operations.
- **QP:** Authorize release or further investigation of contained batches.
